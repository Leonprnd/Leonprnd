# 16 — Prompts voor beeldgeneratie

## Lees dit eerst

De prompts hieronder zijn ingedeeld in drie groepen, en die indeling is geen
formaliteit:

| Groep | Waarvoor | Mag live op de site? |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Productbeeld** | De lamp zelf | **Nee.** Alleen voor moodboards en om je fotograaf te briefen. Het echte beeld maak je met het monster. |
| **B. Sfeer en context** | Situaties waarin het product niet herkenbaar in beeld is | Ja |
| **C. Illustratie en pictogram** | Tekeningen, achtergronden, stappenplannen | Ja |

Waarom groep A niet live mag: een gegenereerde lamp heeft altijd nét een andere
arm, een andere knop of een ander aantal schroeven dan het product dat je
verstuurt. Dat is misleidend en het levert je precies de retouren op die je
probeert te vermijden.

Alle prompts geven een **staand 4:5-beeld** tenzij anders vermeld. Zet je
generator op fotorealistisch en vermijd de standaard "cinematic" en "HDR"-stijlen
— die maken beeld dat er duur uitziet en nep voelt.

## Groep A — Concept en briefing (niet voor de webshop)

### A1 — Hero-concept
```
Photorealistic product photograph of an adjustable desk lamp with a large round
magnifying glass lens (110 mm) in a brushed white metal ring, mounted on an
articulated arm with a table clamp, attached to the edge of a wooden dining
table. The lamp is switched on, casting a clean warm-white pool of light onto a
half-finished crochet blanket in deep navy yarn. Evening: the room behind is
dim, a window shows darkness outside. Shot from a three-quarter angle slightly
above table height, 50 mm lens, f/2.8, natural falloff, no studio flash. Warm
domestic Dutch interior. Muted colour palette: warm off-white, deep green,
natural wood. Slightly underexposed, film-like. No text, no logos, no people.
```

### A2 — Handen onder de lens
```
Close-up photograph of the hands of a woman in her sixties crocheting with a
metal hook and dark blue yarn, seen through and beneath a large round magnifying
lens on an adjustable arm. The hands show age naturally: soft wrinkles, a plain
gold wedding ring, short unpainted nails. Warm task light from above. Shallow
depth of field, focus on the stitches. Evening interior, dim surroundings.
Documentary style, not staged, no eye contact. 85 mm lens, f/2.0. No text.
```

### A3 — Lensdetail
```
Extreme close-up photograph of the edge of a ground glass optical lens set in a
thin brushed metal rim, photographed from a low three-quarter angle so the
thickness and the polished bevel of the glass are visible. A single clean
specular highlight runs across the glass. Dark neutral background, soft raking
light from the left. Macro, 100 mm, f/5.6, tack sharp on the bevel. Product
photography for a precision optics brand. No text, no reflections of a room.
```

## Groep B — Sfeer en context (mag live)

Belangrijk bij deze groep: **het product mag niet herkenbaar in beeld zijn.**
Deze beelden gebruik je voor de herkenningssectie, de gebruikssituaties, de
"over ons"-pagina en als eerste frame van advertenties.

### B1 — Het probleem: te laat op de avond
```
Photograph of a woman in her mid-sixties sitting on a sofa in a Dutch living
room at night, putting down her crochet work in her lap and rubbing the bridge
of her nose with her eyes closed, tired but not distressed. The only light comes
from a warm standing floor lamp two metres away and a television off-frame.
The crochet work is in shadow. Comfortable ordinary interior: a plant, a
bookshelf, a mug on a side table. Documentary photography, available light,
grainy, 35 mm, f/1.8. Muted warm colours. No product visible. No text.
```

### B2 — Vooroverbuigen
```
Photograph from the side of a man in his early seventies leaning forward over a
kitchen table, holding a sheet of paper close to his face and tilting it towards
a distant lamp, squinting slightly. Evening, warm insufficient lighting, long
shadows. Ordinary Dutch kitchen: tiled backsplash, a fruit bowl, reading glasses
pushed up on his forehead. Honest documentary style, no drama, dignified.
35 mm, available light. No product visible. No text.
```

### B3 — Puzzel bij avondlicht
```
Overhead photograph of a partly finished 1000-piece jigsaw puzzle on a dining
table at night, with a pair of hands of an older adult hovering over it holding
one piece. The sky and water sections of the puzzle look almost identical in the
dim warm light. Room lit only by a distant ceiling lamp. Documentary,
available light, slightly underexposed. 35 mm. No product visible. No text.
```

### B4 — Samen aan tafel (cadeauhoek, Q4)
```
Photograph of a woman in her seventies and her adult daughter in her forties
sitting together at a dining table in a Dutch home in December, laughing, a
wrapped brown paper parcel with twine on the table between them. Warm evening
light, candles, a few pine branches. Genuine warmth, not posed, mid-laugh, not
looking at the camera. Documentary family photography, 35 mm, f/2.0, available
light. Muted warm palette. No product visible. No text.
```

### B5 — Achtergrond voor de "over ons"-pagina
```
Photograph of a modest Dutch workshop or office corner: a wooden desk, a few
cardboard shipping boxes stacked neatly, a roll of packing tape, a laptop
closed, a mug. Daylight from a side window. Nothing glamorous, everything real
and slightly worn. Wide shot, 28 mm, natural light, muted colours. No people,
no branding, no text.
```

### B6 — Handen en garen (bannerbeeld)
```
Close-up photograph of the hands of an older woman winding deep green yarn into
a ball, resting in her lap. Soft daylight from a window to the left. Skin shows
age naturally and beautifully. Shallow depth of field. Calm, quiet, warm.
16:9 horizontal. 85 mm, f/2.0. No text, no product.
```

## Groep C — Illustratie en pictogram (mag live)

### C1 — Pictogramset
```
A set of 10 minimal line icons in a single colour (#1F5F4F) on a transparent
background, 2 px uniform stroke weight, rounded caps and joins, 48 x 48 px grid,
geometric and friendly, no fill, no gradients, no shadows. The icons: a desk
lamp, a magnifying glass, a shipping box, a return arrow in a circle, a shield,
a telephone handset, an envelope, a checkmark, a question mark in a circle, a
hand pressing a button. Consistent visual weight across all ten. Flat vector
style, SVG-ready.
```

### C2 — Stappenplan, drie tekeningen
```
Three simple instructional line drawings in a single colour (#1C1B19) on a warm
off-white background (#FAF8F4), 2 px stroke, no shading, technical-manual style
but friendly and hand-drawn in feel. Drawing 1: a clamp being tightened onto the
edge of a table. Drawing 2: a plug being inserted into a wall socket. Drawing 3:
a finger pressing a round button on a lamp head. Uniform style and scale across
all three, generous white space, no text, no numbers, no arrows.
```

### C3 — Uitleg gegoten versus geslepen lens
```
A clean educational diagram on a warm off-white background (#FAF8F4), flat
vector style, two panels side by side. Left panel: a cross-section of a lens
with slightly uneven thickness, with light rays passing through and bending
irregularly at the edges, rays drawn in muted red (#A32B1E). Right panel: a
cross-section of an evenly ground lens with light rays passing through cleanly
and converging at a single point, rays drawn in deep green (#1F5F4F). Thin 2 px
lines, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D. Leave empty space beneath each panel for
a caption to be added later. No text in the image.
```

### C4 — Achtergrondpatroon
```
A very subtle seamless background texture resembling woven natural linen in a
warm off-white tone (#F2EDE4), extremely low contrast, barely visible, no
pattern repeat artefacts, flat and even lighting, suitable as a website section
background behind dark text. 1024 x 1024, tileable.
```

## Verboden in elke prompt

Zet deze woorden nooit in een prompt voor dit merk:

| Niet | Waarom |
|---|---|
| `elderly`, `senior`, `frail`, `helpless`, `struggling` | Levert stereotype beelden van hulpbehoevende ouderen op |
| `smiling at camera`, `thumbs up` | Wordt onmiddellijk als stockbeeld herkend |
| `perfect skin`, `flawless`, `youthful` | We willen echte handen met leeftijd |
| `clinical`, `medical`, `hospital`, `care home` | Duwt het merk de zorglade in |
| `luxury`, `premium`, `high-end`, `cinematic`, `HDR` | Levert glimmend nepbeeld op |
| `vibrant colours`, `saturated` | Botst met het rustige palet |

## Controle vóór publicatie

Voor elk gegenereerd beeld dat live gaat:

- [ ] Staat het product er níet herkenbaar op? (groep B en C)
- [ ] Hebben alle handen vijf vingers en kloppen de gewrichten?
- [ ] Ziet het eruit als een Nederlandse woonkamer, niet als een Amerikaanse?
- [ ] Is het beeld waardig? Zou je het aan je eigen moeder laten zien?
- [ ] Geen onleesbare namaaktekst in beeld?
- [ ] Onder 200 kB na compressie?
